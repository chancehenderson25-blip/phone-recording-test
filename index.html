Phone recording test

Checks whether a web app on this phone can film a long walkaround and keep it safe. Videos stay on this phone. Nothing is uploaded.

Not recording

Steps

  1. In Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it from the Home Screen icon.
  2. Turn the camera on, start recording, walk a car for about 10 minutes and talk as usual.
  3. Partway through: pull down Control Center for 5 seconds. Lock the phone for 10 seconds, then unlock. Switch to another app and back.
  4. Stop. Play it back below. Note any part with no picture or no sound.
  5. Record about 1 minute more, then swipe the app closed without stopping. Open it again: is that recording listed and playable?
  6. Tap "Copy report" and paste it to Claude.
